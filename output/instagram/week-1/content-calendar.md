# TravalBee Instagram — Week 1

## Creative direction

- Format: 4:5 portrait feed post (1080 × 1350)
- Voice: assured, selective, calm, useful
- Visual system: cinematic editorial photography, muted natural color, restrained typography
- Overlay treatment: ivory serif headline with a small emerald accent; keep the TravalBee wordmark and `travalbee.com` in the lower safe zone
- CTA pattern: one clear action per post

## Posting notes

- Recommended sequence: publish in the listed order to alternate destination, brand, and product value.
- Keep text overlays to two short lines and within Instagram’s center safe area.
- Add the product interface as a separate composited layer on Days 4 and 7; do not ask the image model to invent legible UI.
- Use `travalbee.com` consistently in captions and the profile link.
